WikiTree Tree Apps ("the app") is published by **WikiTree** and made by its WikiTree Apps project for Android and
iPhone. It shows the WikiTree Tree Apps from www.wikitree.com on your phone. This policy explains what the
app does with your information. Your use of wikitree.com itself, including the pages the app shows, is
covered by the [WikiTree Privacy Policy](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Help:Privacy) **[confirm link]**.

## In short

- The app has **no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking**, and it contains no
  third-party SDKs.
- Everything the app sends goes to **WikiTree** (www.wikitree.com and api.wikitree.com), and only to
  show you what you asked for.
- Your **WikiTree password is never stored** by the app.
- Your **Saved people, Recent people and the copy of your watchlist stay on your phone.**

## What the app sends to WikiTree

| What | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The WikiTree IDs and names you search for, and the pages and Tree Apps you open | Whenever you use the app | To load the profiles, charts and search results from WikiTree, exactly as a web browser would |
| Your email address and password | Only when you log in | Sent directly to WikiTree's own login, over HTTPS. The app clears the password field straight away and keeps neither. |
| WikiTree login cookies | While you are logged in | To show you the private profiles you are allowed to see and to fetch your watchlist, as on the website |

Like any web server, WikiTree receives your IP address and basic device and browser information with each
request. **[WikiTree: describe server logs and any site analytics here, or link to the section of the
WikiTree Privacy Policy that does.]**

Some Tree Apps load program libraries and fonts from public content networks (cdnjs.cloudflare.com,
cdn.jsdelivr.net, unpkg.com, Google Fonts, Google Hosted Libraries). Those services receive your IP
address when your phone downloads them, as with any website that uses them. A few Tree Apps (such as WT+
Maps) show maps or data from plus.wikitree.com.

## What the app keeps on your phone

| What | Where | How to remove it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved (starred) and Recent people: WikiTree IDs, names and dates | App storage | Remove them in the app, or clear the app's data / delete the app |
| Your WikiTree watchlist (names, IDs, birth and death dates and places), if you are logged in | App storage, shared with the app's home-screen widget | Log out, clear the app's data, or delete the app |
| WikiTree login cookies and the web view's cache | The phone's web view storage | Log out from the ⋮ menu, or delete the app |
| Settings (last person and Tree App, folded sections, landscape choice, Anniversaries reminder on or off) | App storage | Delete the app |
| A picture of a chart you choose to share | Temporary cache, replaced each time | Cleared by the system |

On Android, the system's own backup (if you have it switched on) may include the app's settings and lists
in your Google account backup. Nothing is sent to us.

## Features that use your phone

- **Read aloud** uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The biography text is spoken on the device.
- **Anniversaries reminders** are notifications scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in the app
  or in your phone's settings.
- **Share, Print and Save as PDF** use your phone's own share sheet and print service. Nothing is sent
  anywhere unless you choose a destination.
- The app does **not** use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. WikiTree's own terms set the minimum age for a WikiTree
account.

## Your account and your data on WikiTree

The app does not create or hold accounts of its own. To change or close your WikiTree account, or to ask
about data WikiTree holds, see **[WikiTree's account and privacy help page]** or contact
